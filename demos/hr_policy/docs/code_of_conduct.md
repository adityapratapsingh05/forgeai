# Shore Staff Code of Conduct & Grievance Procedure

## Core Workplace Standards & Conflict of Interest

### Gift Value Threshold
- Employees are prohibited from accepting or offering gifts or gratuities with a value exceeding $50 USD.
- Any gifts or gratuities received must be reported to the Human Resources Department within 7 business days of receipt.

### Outside Employment Approval
- Employees are required to obtain prior approval from the Human Resources Department for any outside employment or consulting activities.
- Approval is required at least 30 days prior to the commencement of any outside employment or consulting activities.

### Confidentiality
- Employees are required to maintain confidentiality regarding proprietary information and trade secrets.
- Any breaches of confidentiality will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

## Anti-Harassment & Whistleblower Protection

### Zero-Tolerance Policy
- Harassment of any kind, including but not limited to sexual harassment, is strictly prohibited.
- Employees found guilty of harassment will face immediate termination of employment.

### Non-Retaliation Window
- Employees who report harassment will not face any retaliation. Any retaliation will be treated as a separate incident and will be handled according to the disciplinary action criteria outlined in this document.

## Formal Grievance Filing & Investigation Timeline

### Filing Deadline
- Employees must file a formal grievance within 30 days of the incident or discovery of the issue.

### Acknowledgment SLA
- The Human Resources Department will acknowledge receipt of a formal grievance within 5 business days of receipt.

### Investigation Completion SLA
- The investigation will be completed within 60 business days of receipt of the grievance.

### Appeal Window
- Employees have the right to appeal the decision within 30 days of receiving the final decision.

## Disciplinary Actions

### Verbal Warning
- Issued for minor infractions or violations of company policies.
- Verbal warnings are issued by the immediate supervisor and documented in the employee's personnel file.

### Written Warning
- Issued for more serious infractions or violations of company policies.
- Written warnings are issued by the Human Resources Department and documented in the employee's personnel file.

### Suspension
- Issued for repeated infractions or violations of company policies.
- Suspensions will be for a minimum of 1 day and a maximum of 30 days, depending on the severity of the infraction.

### Termination
- Issued for severe infractions or violations of company policies, including but not limited to harassment, theft, or fraud.
- Termination will be issued by the Human Resources Department and documented in the employee's personnel file.

---

This document is intended to provide a comprehensive guide for Shore Staff regarding the expected conduct and procedures for handling grievances and disciplinary actions. Employees are expected to adhere to these guidelines to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive work environment.
