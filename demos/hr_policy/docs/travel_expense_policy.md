# Shore Staff Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Flight & Rail Booking Rules
- **Economy Class Flight**: Economy class flights are the standard for all domestic and international travel. Business class flights are reserved for travel that is 8 hours or longer.
- **Advance Booking Lead Days**: For economy class flights, advance booking must be made at least 7 days prior to the travel date. For business class flights, the lead time is 10 days.
- **Flight Hour Threshold**: Business class flights are available for travel that is 12 hours or longer. For flights shorter than 12 hours, economy class must be booked.

## 2. Lodging & Meal Per Diem Caps
- **Domestic Hotel Caps**: The maximum per diem for domestic hotel stays is $200 per night.
- **International Hotel Caps**: The maximum per diem for international hotel stays is $300 per night.
- **Daily Meal Per Diem**: The daily meal per diem for domestic travel is $50, and for international travel, it is $75. Alcohol is excluded from these per diem caps.

## 3. Ground Transport & Mileage
- **Personal Vehicle Rate**: The personal vehicle rate for mileage reimbursement is $0.50 per kilometer.
- **Ride-Sharing vs Rental Car Limits**: For domestic travel, employees are reimbursed for ride-sharing services at the personal vehicle rate. For international travel, rental cars are reimbursed at the personal vehicle rate, but only for distances exceeding 100 kilometers.

## 4. Expense Submission & Audit
- **Receipt Threshold**: Receipts for all expenses must be submitted within 3 days of returning from the trip.
- **Submission Deadline**: Submissions must be made within 5 days of the receipt date.
- **Finance Payout SLA**: Reimbursements will be processed within 7 business days of receipt submission.

---

This policy is designed to ensure that all travel and expense reimbursements are handled efficiently and fairly. Any questions or concerns regarding this policy should be directed to the Human Resources department.
