# Shore Staff Leave & Attendance Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Leave

### Accrual Rate and Maximum Carry-Over
- **Accrual Rate**: For all employees, the annual paid leave accrual rate is 1.5 days per month of service.
- **Maximum Carry-Over Days**: Employees may carry over up to 15 days of unused annual paid leave to the next fiscal year. Any unused leave beyond this cap will be forfeited upon separation.

### Eligibility and Thresholds
- **Eligibility**: All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for annual paid leave.
- **Thresholds**: Employees accrue leave starting from their first day of employment. Employees must provide at least 30 days of continuous service to be eligible for any paid leave.

### Notice Period and Approval Workflow
- **Notice Period**: Employees must give at least 3 business days' notice for any leave request.
- **Approval Workflow**: Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's direct supervisor and then submitted to the Human Resources department for final approval.

### Edge Cases
- **Emergency Leave**: In case of an emergency, employees are required to provide a written statement within 24 hours of the incident.
- **Leave Carry-Over**: If an employee separates from the company, any remaining annual paid leave will be cashed out at the rate of $200 per day.

## 2. Sick & Medical Leave

### Annual Allowance and Medical Certificate Threshold
- **Annual Allowance**: Employees are entitled to a maximum of 10 days of sick leave per year.
- **Medical Certificate**: Employees must provide a medical certificate for any leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Certificates must be provided within 7 days of the leave start date.

### Hospitalization Leave
- **Hospitalization Leave**: Employees who are hospitalized for more than 3 consecutive days are eligible for hospitalization leave, up to a maximum of 10 days per year.

## 3. Parental & Family Leave

### Maternity and Paternity Leave
- **Maternity Weeks**: Employees are entitled to 12 weeks of maternity leave, which can be taken in one continuous period or in two separate periods of up to 6 weeks each.
- **Paternity Days**: Employees are entitled to 3 days of paternity leave, which can be taken in one continuous period.

### Bereavement Leave
- **Bereavement Days**: Employees are entitled to 3 days of bereavement leave for the death of a close family member.

## 4. Public Holidays & Unpaid Leave

### Public Holidays
- **Public Holidays**: Employees are entitled to 10 public holidays per year, which are unpaid.
- **Substitution Window**: Employees can substitute their public holiday entitlement with annual paid leave, provided they have sufficient carry-over.

### Unpaid Leave
- **Unpaid Leave**: Employees are entitled to 5 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. This leave can be taken in one continuous period or in two separate periods of up to 2.5 days each.

## 5. Notice & Approval Procedure

### Notice Periods
- **Short Leave**: For leave of 3 to 14 days, employees must provide at least 2 business days' notice.
- **Long Leave**: For leave of 15 days or more, employees must provide at least 7 business days' notice.

### Approval Workflow
- **Short Leave**: Leave requests for 3 to 14 days are reviewed by the employee's direct supervisor and then submitted to the Human Resources department for final approval.
- **Long Leave**: Leave requests for 15 days or more are reviewed by the employee's direct supervisor, the department head, and then submitted to the Human Resources department for final approval.

### Portal Notice Days
- **Short Leave**: For leave requests of 3 to 14 days, employees must provide notice through the company's internal portal within 2 business days of the leave start date.
- **Long Leave**: For leave requests of 15 days or more, employees must provide notice through the company's internal portal within 7 business days of the leave start date.

---

This policy is subject to change and is intended to provide a clear and comprehensive guide for all Shore employees regarding leave and attendance policies.
