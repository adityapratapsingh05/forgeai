# Vessel Spare Parts Procurement & Requisition Procedure

## Requisition Approval Matrix

| Approval Level | Decision Criteria | Approval Required |
|----------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Superintendent | Parts valued at $10,000 - $50,000 | Superintendent Approval |
| Superintendent | Parts valued at $50,000 or more | Fleet Manager Approval |
| Fleet Manager | Parts valued at $100,000 or more | Technical Director Approval |
| Technical Director | Parts valued at $200,000 or more | CFO Approval |

## Competitive Quotes Rule

- **Single Source OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) Exception:** When a single-source OEM quote is required for critical spare parts, the OEM quote will be considered the final quote.
- **Multiple Quotes Required:** For all other parts, at least three competitive quotes must be obtained before finalizing the purchase. If the lowest quote is within 10% of the second lowest, a third quote must be obtained.

## Emergency / Vessel-Off-Hire Spares Protocol

- **Zero-Stock Escalation:** In the event of a zero-stock situation, the part must be ordered immediately. The part must be delivered within 24 hours.
- **Port-Delivery Cutoff Hours:** For parts requiring urgent delivery, the port delivery cutoff hours are 12:00 PM (noon) daily. Parts ordered after this time will be delivered the following business day.

## Goods Receipt & Invoice Matching

- **3-Way Match Tolerance:** The tolerance for the 3-way match (invoice, packing slip, and goods receipt) is 2%. Any discrepancies must be reported within 5 working days.
- **Discrepancy Reporting:** Any discrepancies found after the 3-way match should be reported to the Procurement Department within 5 working days. The Procurement Department will then escalate the issue to the appropriate department for resolution.
