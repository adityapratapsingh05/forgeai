## General Terms
- **General Payment Terms:** All payments are due within the stated payment terms. Late payments will incur a 2% finance charge per month.
- **Currency:** All payments must be made in the currency specified in the invoice.
- **Warranty:** Warranty claims must be submitted within 12 months of delivery. Claims outside this period will not be honored.
- **Express Air-Freight Surcharges:** For urgent orders, the surcharge will be applied to the total order value, including any applicable volume discounts and handling fees.
- **Hazardous / Cold-Chain Parts Handling:** Packaging surcharges are calculated based on the order value and are non-refundable. Orders below the minimum order value will incur a handling fee of 1% of the order value.
